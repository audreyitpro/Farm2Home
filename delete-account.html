Account & Data Deletion

Request Account Deletion

Farm2Home Direct users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Option 1 — Delete Through the App

If account deletion functionality is available for your account type, sign in to Farm2Home Direct and open your Profile or Settings section.

Select the account deletion option and follow the displayed instructions.

Option 2 — Submit an Account Deletion Request

If you cannot access the application, send an email to:

audreyitprofessional@gmail.com

Use the subject:

Farm2Home Direct Account Deletion Request

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your Farm2Home Direct account
  • Your account type: Customer, Farmer, Freight Carrier, or Driver
  • Your request to delete your Farm2Home Direct account

Do not send your password, payment card information, Social Security number, or other sensitive authentication information by email.

Information That May Be Deleted

Depending on the account and applicable requirements, deletion may include:

  • Account profile information
  • Stored contact information
  • Marketplace profile information
  • Saved application preferences
  • Location-related account information where applicable
  • Uploaded content that is not required to be retained

Information That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained when required for legitimate business, legal, security, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, regulatory, transaction, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Processing

Farm2Home Direct will review account deletion requests and may contact the requester to verify account ownership before completing deletion.
