Farm2Home Direct

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

Farm2Home Direct respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Farm2Home Direct collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. About Farm2Home Direct

Farm2Home Direct is a marketplace and logistics platform that connects customers, farmers, agricultural businesses, freight carriers, and delivery drivers.

Features may include farm marketplace shopping, product listings, customer orders, pickup and delivery coordination, freight load management, driver delivery tools, payment processing, payouts, notifications, and location-based delivery functionality.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account identifiers
  • Business or farm information
  • Billing and transaction information
  • Order and purchase history
  • Pickup and delivery addresses
  • Approximate or precise location
  • Device and notification information
  • Product and marketplace information
  • Photos uploaded for products, profiles, pickup, or proof of delivery
  • Compliance or business documents voluntarily uploaded by business users
  • Customer support communications

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts
  • Operate the Farm2Home Direct marketplace
  • Process and manage orders
  • Support pickup, delivery, and freight workflows
  • Provide location-enabled driver and delivery features
  • Send important account, order, delivery, and service notifications
  • Process eligible payments, subscriptions, and payouts
  • Maintain platform security
  • Prevent fraud and misuse
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve application functionality

4. User Roles

Farm2Home Direct supports multiple account types, which may include:

  • Customers
  • Farmers and agricultural businesses
  • Freight carriers
  • Delivery drivers

Different information may be required depending on the account type and features being used.

5. Location Information

Farm2Home Direct may request access to approximate or precise location for features such as delivery routing, live driver location, pickup coordination, delivery tracking, and nearby marketplace functionality.

Location information is only used in connection with applicable application features and device permissions.

6. Camera and Photos

Farm2Home Direct may request access to your camera or photo library for features including product photos, profile images, proof of pickup, proof of delivery, compliance documentation, or other user-requested uploads.

7. Payments and Payouts

Farm2Home Direct may use Stripe and Stripe Connect to support payment, subscription, connected-account, and payout functionality.

Payment card information may be collected and processed directly by Stripe. Farm2Home Direct does not intend to store complete payment card numbers on its own application servers.

8. Third-Party Services

Farm2Home Direct may use service providers including:

  • Supabase for authentication, application data, databases, and storage
  • Stripe and Stripe Connect for payment and payout functionality
  • Expo and React Native technologies for mobile application functionality
  • Mapping and location services for applicable delivery and navigation features
  • Notification services for account, order, freight, and delivery updates

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable service agreements.

9. Information Sharing

Farm2Home Direct does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide requested marketplace, payment, order, delivery, or freight services.

For example, appropriate order or delivery information may be made available to participating customers, farmers, carriers, or drivers involved in a transaction or delivery.

Information may also be disclosed when legally required or when necessary to protect the security, rights, and safety of Farm2Home Direct, its users, or others.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain accounts, complete transactions, resolve disputes, meet legal or regulatory obligations, prevent fraud, and enforce agreements.

12. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Farm2Home Direct account and associated personal information.

Account deletion may be initiated through applicable account settings within the app or through our external account deletion request page:

Farm2Home Direct Account Deletion

Certain information may be retained when required for legal, transaction, security, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, regulatory, or dispute-resolution purposes.

13. Children's Privacy

Farm2Home Direct is not directed to children under 13. Business, payment, delivery-driver, and freight-carrier functionality is intended for eligible adult users.

14. United States Availability

Farm2Home Direct is currently designed primarily for users and participating businesses in the United States. Marketplace inventory, participating farms, delivery services, and freight availability may vary by geographic area.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
